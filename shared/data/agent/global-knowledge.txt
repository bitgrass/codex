Bitgrass Agent Global Knowledge

Use this file for persistent product knowledge and operating rules the agent should follow.
Keep entries short and factual.

Core rules
- Portfolio includes a Transactions tab (third tab).
- Users can check transactions in Portfolio -> Transactions and in wallet Activity/BaseScan.
- "Own Plot" is for discovering and buying plots, not for managing already-owned assets.
- Owned plots are managed from Portfolio (NFTs tab).
- For direct read requests (balance, earnings, NFTs), execute intent.
- For how-to/informational requests, explain without executing write transactions.

Style rules
- Keep answers concise and practical.
- Prefer product-accurate answers over generic crypto explanations.

Tier reference
- Standard
- Size: 100m²
- Price: 0.05 ETH
- Supply: 2000 NFTs
- Carbon removal potential: up to 0.1 tCO2/year
- Early-adopter leaderboard reward: 5,000 BTG per NFT

- Premium

- Carbon removal potential: up to 0.1 tCO2/year
- Early-adopter leaderboard reward: 20,000 BTG per NFT

- Legendary
- Size: 1000m²
- Price: 0.35 ETH
- Supply: 400 NFTs
- Carbon removal potential: up to 0.1 tCO2/year
- Early-adopter leaderboard reward: 35,000 BTG per NFT

BCO2 guidance
- BCO2 is your carbon-credit reward stream tied to tokenized land plots and their carbon-removal potential.
- Earning flow (simple):
- 1) Own a plot: Standard, Premium, or Legendary.
- 2) Stake it: open the Earn BCO2 (staking) page and stake your plot.
- 3) Accrue rewards: BCO2 builds over time while your plot is staked.
- 4) Claim anytime: claim your available BCO2 from the staking flow.
- 5) Repeat and scale: stake more plots to increase your potential rewards.
- If users ask "how to earn BCO2", explain the 5 steps above in short, practical language.
- If users ask tier differences, always include size, price, supply, and carbon-removal potential.
